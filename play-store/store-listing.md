## Full Description

i-Sight Med lets you connect a compatible USB camera to your Android device and use it as a simple local camera viewer.

Use i-Sight Med to:

- Preview video from supported USB/UVC cameras.
- Capture photos from the connected camera.
- Record videos locally on your device.
- Choose where captures are saved.

i-Sight Med is designed for local use. The app does not require login, does not include ads, and does not upload your photos, videos, or audio to a server.

Permissions:

- Camera: used to access and preview the connected camera.
- Microphone: used only when recording video with audio from the device microphone.
- Storage/media access: used to save or access captures on your device.
- USB host access: used to connect to compatible USB cameras.

Compatibility depends on your Android device, USB adapter, and USB camera support.
