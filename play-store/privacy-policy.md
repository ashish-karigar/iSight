# Privacy Policy for i-Sight Med

Effective date: June 11, 2026

i-Sight Med is a local USB camera viewer, photo capture, and video recording app for Android.

## Information We Collect

i-Sight Med does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with us or third parties.

The app can access camera, microphone, USB device, and local storage features on your device only to provide its core functionality:

- Camera and USB access are used to preview compatible USB cameras.
- Microphone access is used only when recording video with audio from the device microphone.
- Storage or media access is used to save and access photos and videos on your device.

Photos, videos, and audio recordings are stored locally on your device or in the folder you choose. They are not uploaded by i-Sight.

## Internet Access

i-Sight Med does not require internet access for its core functionality.

## Third Parties

i-Sight Med does not include advertising, analytics, account login, or cloud upload services.

## Children

i-Sight Med is not specifically directed to children.

## Data Deletion

Because i-Sight Med stores captures locally on your device, you can delete photos and videos directly from your device storage or from the folder where you saved them.

## Changes

We may update this privacy policy if the app changes. Any updates should be reflected on this page.
